Patients from abroad

From any city, state or country.

Some of the clinic's patients do not live in São Paulo. The care is the same, with one extra step of planning so that the consultation, the journey, the surgery and the recovery happen in the right order and with as little travel as possible.

How it works

In six steps.

The first contact already organises the rest. Nothing is scheduled before a conversation with the physician.

First contact

By WhatsApp or phone, in Portuguese or English. Tell us where you are writing from, which specialty you are looking for and, if you wish, send recent test results. The team replies with date options and the format of the first consultation.

First consultation: in person or online

The first consultation can be in person, in São Paulo, or online, by video call, when the case allows a remote assessment. The physician examines or advises, says whether there is an indication and whether travelling is worthwhile, and when.

Tests and planning

With the indication defined, the clinic organises the list of tests, which can be done in your home city or in São Paulo, and builds the calendar: arrival, in-person consultation, surgery or procedure and return visits, on nearby dates.

In-person consultation and surgery

Those who started online have their in-person consultation right on arrival, for examination and confirmation of the plan. Surgery takes place at Hospital Vila Nova Star, with the clinic's team; dermatological procedures, at the clinic itself.

Your stay in São Paulo

The length of stay is defined case by case, according to the procedure and the expected recovery. The clinic suggests nearby accommodation and advises on transport and on bringing a companion when the procedure requires it.

Follow-up

In-person return visits are concentrated in the period the patient is in the city. Afterwards, follow-up continues remotely, by video call and WhatsApp, when the case allows, with the same team.

Communication

Language and contact

  • Care in Portuguese and English
  • WhatsApp contact during São Paulo business hours (GMT-3)
  • Video calls for the initial assessment and for return visits, when indicated

Your stay

Accommodation and logistics

  • The clinic is in Vila Nova Conceição, a residential neighbourhood with hotels and services a few minutes away
  • Accommodation suggestions on request
  • Guidance on transport, companions and rest, depending on the procedure
  • Tests and return visits concentrated in a few days, to reduce travel
