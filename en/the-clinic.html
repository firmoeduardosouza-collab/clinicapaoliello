The clinic

Hospital rigour, personal scale.

Paoliello Clinic is a private medical clinic in Vila Nova Conceição, São Paulo, where four specialties work under one standard: precise diagnosis, shared decisions and continuous follow-up. Consultations and dermatological procedures happen here; surgery, at Hospital Vila Nova Star.

How we think

Time, shared decisions and continuity.

Three principles that apply to every specialty and every patient, from São Paulo or from abroad.

01

Time

Serious medicine takes time. Appointments are scheduled with an interval between them, and the first conversation is long: examination, listening, questions and a clear explanation of the options, including the option not to intervene.

Nobody leaves the first consultation with surgery booked. They leave with a diagnosis, a path and as much time as they need to decide.

02

Shared decisions

Decisions are made with the patient. When treatment or surgery is indicated, the plan states what will be done, where, by whom and what recovery will look like. Nothing is decided on behalf of the person who will live the process.

Risks, limits and alternatives are stated beforehand. We do not promise results and we do not publish before-and-after comparisons: each case is discussed individually, in consultation.

03

Continuity

The same team follows every step. Whoever leads the consultation leads the treatment and the follow-up visits. When a case involves more than one specialty, the teams talk to each other, at the same address.

Plastic surgery, dermatology, colorectal surgery and nutrition exchange information about the same patient without the patient having to retell the story at every door.

Where it happens

At the clinic and in hospital.

What is outpatient happens here. What is surgical happens in a reference hospital, with the same team.

At the clinic

Consultations, procedures and follow-up

  • Consultations in all specialties, in person or online as an initial assessment
  • Pre-operative assessment and guidance
  • Dermatological procedures, in the treatment room
  • Return visits and post-operative follow-up
  • Nutrition consultations

In hospital

Surgery

  • Plastic, general and colorectal surgery performed at Hospital Vila Nova Star, São Paulo
  • Operating theatres, anaesthesia and hospital admission
  • Laparoscopic and robotic surgery, when indicated

The space

Quiet, light and materials.

White render, wood, ceramic and marble. Small rooms, natural light, appointments with a set time and few people at once.

Team

Specialists who work together.

Three plastic surgeons, a dermatologist, a general and colorectal surgeon and a nutritionist. Each with a recognised title and a defined focus; between them, a case moves naturally when it needs more than one pair of eyes.

The plastic surgeons are members of the Brazilian Society of Plastic Surgery. Colorectal surgery is backed by robotic surgery certification. Dermatology performs its procedures at the clinic itself.

Meet the team

Privacy

Few people at a time.

Appointments with a set time, small waiting rooms and separate spaces for consultations and procedures. Patients from other cities or countries receive the same care, with one extra step of planning.
