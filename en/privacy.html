Privacy policy

How we handle the personal data of people who contact Paoliello Clinic.

Last updated: September 2026.
This text must be reviewed by the clinic's legal counsel before publication.

Who we are

TPAA Médicos Associados LTDA, CNPJ 42.313.789/0001-80, based at Rua João Lourenço, 306, Vila Nova Conceição, São Paulo, SP, 04029-100, Brazil, responsible for Paoliello Clinic and for this website.

What data we collect

This website uses no forms and no tracking cookies. When you contact us by WhatsApp or phone, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, phone number and the reason for contact. Health information is only processed in the context of medical care, with the additional protection provided by law.

What we use it for

  • Replying to your message and scheduling consultations
  • Organising medical care and follow-up
  • Meeting legal and regulatory obligations, including those of the Brazilian Federal Council of Medicine

Who we share it with

Data is accessed only by the clinic's staff and by the professionals involved in your care. When surgery is performed in hospital, the necessary information is shared with the institution, as required by health legislation. We do not sell or transfer data to third parties for commercial purposes.

How long we keep it

Contact data is kept for as long as there is a relationship with the clinic or a legal obligation to retain it. Medical records follow the retention periods set by legislation and by the Federal Council of Medicine.

Your rights

You may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymisation, portability or deletion of your data, as well as information about sharing, under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018). To exercise these rights, contact the clinic through its channels.

Third-party services

This website loads typefaces from Google servers. Links to WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Maps lead to services with their own privacy policies.
