Specialty

Colorectal and General Surgery

Diagnosis and treatment of diseases of the bowel, colon, rectum and anus, with minimally invasive and robotic surgery.

Coloproctology deals with diseases of the large bowel, rectum and anus, such as haemorrhoids, fissures, fistulas, inflammatory bowel disease and colorectal tumours. At Paoliello Clinic the specialty is led by Dr. Marina Gimenez, a general and colorectal surgeon certified in robotic surgery, trained at St Mark's Hospital in London.

Consultations and follow-up take place at the clinic, in a private setting. When surgery is indicated, the procedure is performed in hospital, favouring laparoscopic and robotic techniques, which reduce surgical trauma and support recovery.
