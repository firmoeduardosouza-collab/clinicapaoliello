Specialty

Nutrition

Clinical nutrition integrated with pre- and post-operative care, body composition and gut health, for all genders and stages of life.

Nutrition at Paoliello Clinic works alongside the other specialties. Before surgery it prepares the body; afterwards it supports recovery and healing. Outside the surgical context it follows body composition, gut health and the needs of each stage of life, always with individual plans reviewed over time.

Consultations take place at the clinic, at the same address where patients are followed by the medical teams. When a case involves dermatology or plastic surgery, the nutrition plan is built together with the responsible physician.
