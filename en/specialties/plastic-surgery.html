Specialty

Plastic Surgery

Body contouring, breast, face and intimate surgery. Consultations and follow-up at the clinic; surgery performed at Hospital Vila Nova Star.

Plastic surgery at Paoliello Clinic begins with a long consultation: examination, listening and a clear explanation of the options, including the option not to operate. When surgery is indicated, the plan is built together with the patient and the procedure takes place at Hospital Vila Nova Star in São Paulo, with a full surgical and anaesthetic team. Pre- and post-operative care happens at the clinic, with the same team.

Three plastic surgeons see patients here, each with a defined focus: body contouring and breast, facial rejuvenation, and facial and body contouring. All are members of the Brazilian Society of Plastic Surgery. Every indication is individual; risks, limits and alternatives are discussed in consultation.

Procedures

The procedures below are performed by the clinic's team in hospital. Each indication depends on assessment in consultation; risks, limits and alternatives are discussed individually.

Body

Liposuction

Dr. Thiago Paoliello

Localised removal of fat to redefine the contour of areas such as the abdomen, flanks, back and thighs. Conventional or high-definition, depending on the indication.

High-definition liposuction

Dr. Thiago Paoliello

A technique that, beyond removing fat, highlights the contour of the underlying muscles with the help of specific technologies. Indicated in selected cases, after assessment.

Abdominoplasty

Dr. Thiago Paoliello

Removal of excess skin and fat from the abdomen, with repositioning of the abdominal muscles when indicated, and reconstruction of the abdominal contour.

Skin removal of arms, thighs and back

Dr. Thiago Paoliello

Removal of excess skin from the arms, legs and back, common after major weight loss.

Combined body contouring surgery

Dr. Thiago Paoliello · Dr. Vicente Andrade

Joint planning of liposuction, abdominoplasty and other procedures when more than one area is to be treated in the same operation.

Breast

Breast surgery (mammoplasty)

Dr. Thiago Paoliello

Breast augmentation, reduction or lift, with individual planning of shape and proportion and, when possible, reduced-scar techniques.

Face

Deep plane facelift

Dr. Mariana Genelhu

A facial rejuvenation technique that repositions the deep structures of the face, with attention to natural expression.

Blepharoplasty

Dr. Thiago Paoliello

Eyelid surgery to remove excess skin and fat pockets, with an effect on the gaze and, when indicated, on the field of vision.

Intimate

Labiaplasty

Dr. Thiago Paoliello

Reduction of the labia minora, for functional or aesthetic reasons, performed in hospital.
