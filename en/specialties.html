Specialties

What we treat, and where.

Four specialties under one roof and one standard. Consultations, dermatological procedures and follow-up take place at the clinic. Surgery, at Hospital Vila Nova Star.

01

Plastic Surgery

Body contouring, breast, face and intimate surgery. Conventional and high-definition liposuction, abdominoplasty, skin-removal procedures, breast surgery, deep plane facelift, blepharoplasty and labiaplasty.

At the clinic, or online for patients from abroad

Dermatology

Colorectal and General Surgery

Diseases of the bowel, colon, rectum and anus: haemorrhoids, fissures, fistulas, inflammatory bowel disease and colorectal tumours. Laparoscopic and robotic surgery in hospital.
