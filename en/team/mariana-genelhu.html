Plastic Surgery

Dra. Mariana Genelhu

Plastic surgeon

CRM-SP 173.228 · RQE 86.601

Plastic surgeon and full member of the Brazilian Society of Plastic Surgery. Her practice is dedicated to facial rejuvenation, with expertise in the deep plane facelift, a technique that repositions the deep structures of the face rather than simply pulling the skin.

In consultation she starts from listening and examination to explain what is possible and what is not, with attention to natural expression and the identity of each face. Surgery is performed in hospital; follow-up takes place at the clinic.

  • Surgical planning and pre- and post-operative care
