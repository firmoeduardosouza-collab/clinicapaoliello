How it works

The consultation, in three stages

Every relationship with the clinic starts with a consultation. That is where it is decided, together with the patient, whether there is an indication, what makes sense and at what pace.

01

First conversation

The first consultation is long and does not end with a mandatory decision. The physician examines, listens to what bothers you and what you expect, and explains the options with the same clarity for a yes and for a no. Patients from other cities or countries can start with an online assessment.

Recent test results and a list of the medication you take help; if there are none, the requests are issued right there.

02

Plan

When there is an indication, the plan is written together: what will be done, where, by whom, with what preparation and what recovery will look like. Risks, limits and alternatives are stated before, not after.

Surgery takes place at Hospital Vila Nova Star, with a full surgical and anaesthetic team. Dermatological procedures and pre-operative care, at the clinic. Nutrition joins the preparation when it makes a difference.

03

Follow-up

Return visits and post-operative care take place at the clinic, with the same team that handled the case. WhatsApp stays open for questions between one visit and the next.

For those who live far away, in-person visits are concentrated in the period spent in São Paulo, and follow-up continues remotely when the case allows.
